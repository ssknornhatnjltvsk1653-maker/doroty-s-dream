# Doroty's Dream

Create a beautiful, highly polished romantic proposal website dedicated to a girl named Doroty.



The website should feel like a dreamy pink fairytale love story — elegant, emotional, feminine, magical, and premium. It should NOT look like a generic Valentine's Day template.



Overall aesthetic



- Main colors: soft pink, blush pink, white, rose red, subtle champagne

- Lots of beautiful roses and delicate flower petals

- Princess/fairytale atmosphere

- Soft glassmorphism

- Elegant typography with a romantic serif font for headings and a clean font for body text

- Rounded cards and buttons

- Soft glow, subtle shadows and beautiful gradients

- Floating hearts, roses, sparkles and petals

- Smooth animations throughout

- Fully responsive and optimized for mobile because she will probably open it on her phone

- Make it feel luxurious and cinematic rather than childish



Opening screen



Start with a gorgeous animated pink background with floating rose petals.



In the center show:



"Doroty..."



Then:



"I made a little something for you ♡"



Add a beautiful animated button:



"Open My Heart ♡"



When she clicks it, transition smoothly into the main experience with petals and a soft glow animation.



Section 1 — My Favorite Person



Create a beautiful section introducing Doroty.



Text:



"Out of all the people in this world, somehow I got lucky enough to find you."



Make the section visually emotional with roses slowly appearing around the text.



Section 2 — Things That Make Me Think Of You



Create floating interactive cards representing things she loves:



🌹 Red roses

🎀 Pink everything

🐰 Bunnies

👑 Princesses

🎮 Video games

🎵 Music

🛍️ Shopping

🌙 Sleeping

✍️ Poetry



Each card should have a cute hover/tap animation.



Section 3 — A Little Poem



Create an elegant handwritten-style poem section.



Title:



"For the girl who made my world pink"



Use a beautiful original romantic poem about Doroty, roses, pink skies, feeling lucky to have her, and how she makes ordinary moments feel special.



Keep it heartfelt and genuine rather than overly dramatic.



Section 4 — Our Little World



Create an interactive dreamy timeline/story section.



Use placeholders where I can later add:



- Our first conversation

- My favorite memory with you

- The moment I realized I loved you

- A special moment

- Our future together



Make each moment appear with beautiful animations as the user scrolls.



Section 5 — Music



Create a beautiful music-player-style section called:



"Songs that feel like you ♡"



Use a dreamy album-player design.



Include placeholders for songs rather than automatically embedding copyrighted music.



The visual style should feel inspired by the dreamy atmosphere of Lana Del Rey and the darker stylish aesthetic associated with Playboi Carti, without copying either artist's branding.



Section 6 — The Love Letter



Create a realistic-looking romantic letter/card.



Heading:



"Dear Doroty,"



Add a heartfelt original love letter explaining how special she is to me, how much I appreciate her, and how she has become such an important part of my world.



Make the letter appear gradually as the user scrolls.



Final Proposal



This should be the most beautiful part of the entire website.



Transition into a magical rose garden / pink dreamy background.



Show:



"Doroty..."



Then:



"I don't need a perfect world."



"I just want a world where I get to keep loving you."



Then a large animated question:



"Will you be mine? ♡"



Add two buttons:



YES ♡



OF COURSE 🎀



Do NOT make the NO button frustrating, impossible to click, or manipulate the user. Keep both choices respectful and functional.



After YES



When she clicks YES:



- Trigger a beautiful rose-petal celebration

- Pink hearts and sparkles float across the screen

- Add a subtle celebratory animation

- Play a soft non-copyrighted celebration sound if available, with a mute option

- Reveal:



"You just made my whole world even more beautiful. ♡"



Then:



"I love you, Doroty."



Add a final animated rose blooming.



Important UX requirements



- Mobile-first

- Extremely smooth scrolling

- Beautiful page transitions

- No unnecessary navigation bar

- No clutter

- No excessive text

- Make every section feel like part of one continuous love story

- Add a small music/mute control if background audio is implemented

- Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings

- Make loading fast

- Use high-quality royalty-free imagery or CSS-generated visuals

- Avoid copyrighted images

- Make all animations smooth and subtle

- The final result should feel like a premium interactive digital proposal/love letter made specifically for Doroty.



Build the complete working website, not just a static mockup. Make every button and interaction functional.
